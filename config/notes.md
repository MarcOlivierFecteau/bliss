### `Mode` button

The `Mode` toggle button swaps the left joystick and the D-Pad's indices. In other words:

- LJH <---> DPH;
- LJV <---> DPV.

Additionally, the left joystick now behaves as a D-Pad.

This feature works the same for both X-Mode and D-Mode.
